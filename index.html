About

I enjoy taking robotic systems from an idea to a working prototype.

I am a graduate researcher in Mechanical Engineering at Carnegie Mellon University. My interests span robot design, locomotion, sensing, control, embedded systems, and state estimation.

I am especially interested in projects that require close integration between hardware and software, where mechanical design, sensing, and control have to be developed together for the robot to work reliably.

Research

Selected research

Research across legged, underwater, aerial, and bio-inspired robotic systems.

Current-based proprioception · Small-scale locomotion

Little Quadruped Robot

A fully untethered centimeter-scale quadruped that uses motor current as a source of proprioceptive leg feedback. A neural-network classifier estimates stance and swing, providing contact events for closed-loop gait coordination without joint encoders or dedicated foot-contact sensors.

Motor current sensing Embedded systems Machine learning MuJoCo

Underwater robotics · State estimation

HUMRS

Research on an articulated underwater robot involving ROS integration, vision-based state estimation, and mechanical design for underwater operation. The work combines system integration with practical design considerations for reliable testing in water.

ROS VINS-Fusion Vision-based state estimation Waterproof design

Aerial self-righting · Bio-inspired robotics

Cat-Inspired Robot

A bio-inspired robot that combines an actuated spine and tail for aerial self-righting. The platform is used to study how multiple body-reorientation strategies can work together during rapid recovery maneuvers.

Self-righting Reinforcement learning MuJoCo Mechanical design

Decentralized coordination · Bio-inspired control

Whole-Body Quadruped Coordination

Research on emergent inter-limb and body–limb coordination using decentralized control. The work explores how coordinated locomotion can arise without prescribing complete foot trajectories.

CPG GRF feedback Whole-body coordination

Self-Balancing Motorcycle with Reaction Wheel
